Boat rental in Istanbul

tekne.istanbul brings boat and yacht rental, private Bosphorus tours and boat maintenance together in one place. Pick a date and send a request for a skippered boat, an hourly yacht charter, a birthday or marriage proposal on a boat, a breakfast cruise or a moonlight tour on the Bosphorus.

In summer we also offer jet ski, speedboat and rowing boat rentals. For boat owners, we arrange periodic maintenance, antifouling, winterizing and reliable technicians for engine, electrical and plumbing work.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to pay to book?+

No. The request you send on the site is a pre-booking. Once we confirm availability we call you and agree on the price and payment method by phone.

Where do the boats depart from?+

Depending on the service and boat, departures are possible from piers on both sides of the Bosphorus such as Kuruçeşme, Bebek, Arnavutköy, Karaköy, Kadıköy and Üsküdar. We choose the closest point together.

Do the boats come with a captain?+

Yes, all yacht and boat rentals include a captain and crew. Jet ski and rowing boat rentals include life jackets and a safety briefing.

What if the weather is bad?+

If wind or rain makes the trip unsafe, we set a new date together with you.
